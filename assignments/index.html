    Assignments — Overview & Grading¶

    All assignments are submitted on Canvas. This site contains the full instructions and rubrics. When in doubt about a deadline or grade, check Canvas or ask your TA.

    Grading Summary¶

    Ongoing Coursework — 60%¶

    ID Assignment Type Cadence Weight Submit to
    A1 Weekly Reflection Individual Weekly · best 10 of 12 10% Canvas — text entry
    A2 Weekly 4-Box Report Team Weekly · best 10 of 12 20% Canvas — text entry
    A3 Peer Evaluation Individual Wks 6 & 12 20% Canvas — quiz
    A4 Tech Lead Check-in Attendance Individual Weekly · 10 of 12 required 10% Logged by Tech Lead
    A5 Stakeholder Meeting Attendance Individual Weekly · 10 of 12 required 10% Logged by Tech Lead

    Final Deliverables — 40%¶

    All due Monday, Dec 7 (Demo Day). Submitted on Canvas.

    ID Assignment Type Weight Submit to
    F1 Working AI Tool Team 10% Canvas — link to GitHub repo
    F2 Technical Documentation & User Guide Team 10% Canvas — upload or link
    F3 Final Presentation & Demo Team 10% Canvas — video + slides
    F4 Client Handoff Package Team 10% Canvas — link to drive/repo

    Drop & Absence Policy¶

    Assignment Policy
    A1 — Reflections Best 10 of 12 count. You can miss 2 with no penalty.
    A2 — 4-Box Best 10 of 12 count. You can miss 2 with no penalty.
    A3 — Peer Eval Both required. No drops.
    A4 — Tech Lead 10 of 12 required. 2 allowed absences.
    A5 — Stakeholder 10 of 12 required. 2 allowed absences.
    F1–F4 All required. No drops.

    Late Policy¶

    When Penalty
    Within 72 hours of deadline −20% on earned score
    More than 72 hours late 0 points

    Extension requests must reach your TA at least 24 hours before the deadline and require documentation of exceptional circumstances.

    Team vs. Individual¶

    • Team submissions: one person submits on behalf of the team; all members share the grade. Sprint scores may be adjusted individually based on contribution.
    • Individual submissions: you submit your own work; you receive your own grade.
