# A5 — Stakeholder Meeting Attendance

!!! abstract "Submission details"
    **Type:** Individual · **Weight:** 10% of final grade  
    **Due:** Weekly (best 10 of 12 required)  
    **Format:** No student submission — score recorded in Canvas by your TA  
    **Where:** Canvas → Grades → Stakeholder Meeting Attendance

---

## Overview

Each project team holds a **weekly meeting with their stakeholders (clients) and Tech Lead**. These are your primary touchpoints with the people your tool is built for — where you gather requirements, demo progress, collect feedback, and manage expectations.

These meetings are **client-facing.** Your client is a real Cornell stakeholder giving real time and trust to your team's work. The bar is higher than a Tech Lead check-in: arriving late, being disengaged, or showing up unprepared in front of a client doesn't just affect your grade — it shapes your client's confidence in the team. **Treat each one as a professional working session with an external partner.**

**Best 10 of 12 attendance grades count toward your final grade.** You have 2 allowed absences.

---

## What These Meetings Cover

- **Sprint progress update:** what your team built since the last meeting and what's coming next
- **Demo of working features:** show the client what the tool currently does
- **Requirement clarification:** resolve open questions about scope, data, or user needs
- **Feedback collection:** gather the client's reactions and capture action items
- **Risk and blocker discussion:** surface anything that could derail the next sprint

---

## What to Prepare Each Week

- Knowledge of your team's current sprint status (what shipped, what's in progress)
- One specific demo, screenshot, or artifact you can show or reference
- Open questions you need the client to answer
- Notes from the **previous** client meeting — what did the client ask for, and where does it stand?

---

## Grading Rubric

Each weekly stakeholder meeting is worth 10 points. Your Tech Lead assigns a tier based on observation; your TA enters it in Canvas. Best 10 of 12 weeks count.

The rubric considers five dimensions together: **punctuality**, **preparation** (knowing your team's status), **contribution** (presenting your part, asking client-facing questions), **engagement** (listening, capturing feedback), and **follow-through** (acting on what the client asked for last week). The score is holistic.

=== "10 — Exemplary"
    On time. Knows the team's current status cold. Presents their part of the work clearly to the client. Asks the client at least one specific, useful question that moves the project forward. Captures client feedback in real time and confirms action items before the meeting ends. Has visible follow-through on something the client asked for in a prior meeting.

=== "9 — Strong"
    On time. Prepared with team status and able to present their portion. Engages with client feedback substantively. Asks at least one good clarifying question. Has done work since last meeting that connects to something the client raised.

=== "8 — Solid"
    On time. Knows enough to contribute when called on. Listens attentively when client speaks. Doesn't fumble but doesn't drive the conversation either. Has clearly done work but isn't tracking client priorities specifically.

=== "7 — Adequate"
    On time but contributes minimally to the client conversation. When asked to speak, gives a generic update. Doesn't ask the client questions. Engagement during client feedback is passive (no notes, no follow-ups).

=== "6 — Weak"
    Arrives 10+ minutes late or leaves 10+ minutes early without prior notice. Or is visibly disengaged in front of the client (camera off, no contributions). Or clearly hasn't tracked the project this week and has nothing to say when called on.

=== "0 — Absent"
    Did not attend. You have 2 excused absences per semester — they function as the 2 dropped scores in the best-10-of-12 calculation.

---

!!! info "If you must miss a sprint meeting"
    Notify your **TA and Tech Lead at least 2 hours in advance.** This is common courtesy and helps the team plan around your absence. Pre-notified absences may or may not count toward your attendance grade — check the syllabus and talk to your TA.
