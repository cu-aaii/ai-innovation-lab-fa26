# A3 — Peer Evaluation

!!! abstract "Submission details"
    **Type:** Individual · **Weight:** 20% of final grade (10 pts each × 2)  
    **Due:** Midterm — Fri Oct 30 · Final — Mon Dec 7  
    **Format:** Quiz on Canvas (no file upload)  
    **Where:** Canvas → Assignments → Peer Evaluation (Mid or Final)

---

## Overview

Twice per semester you will complete a short peer evaluation about your team's collaboration. It is structured reflection on **how your team is working together** and what you can each do to improve. Your responses help the instructor and TAs identify teams that need support early.

**Confidentiality:** Your responses are shared only with the instructor and TA — never with your teammates.

---

## What the Evaluation Covers

- Your own contribution to the team (tasks completed, reliability, communication)
- How well your team is dividing work and supporting each other
- Specific behaviors you have observed — both positive and areas for growth
- What you personally commit to doing differently in the next half of the semester

---

## Survey Questions

For each **teammate**, you will rate on a 1–5 scale and answer two open-ended prompts:

1. This person consistently showed up prepared and on time for team work. *(1–5)*
2. This person communicated blockers and updates clearly and proactively. *(1–5)*
3. This person pulled their weight — their commits, reviews, and contributions matched their commitments. *(1–5)*
4. This person made the team more effective. *(1–5)*
5. What's one thing this person does especially well as a collaborator? *(text)*
6. What's one thing this person could do to be a more effective teammate? *(text)*

Plus three **self-reflection** prompts:

7. Describe one specific moment when your team collaborated well. What made it work? *(text)*
8. Describe one specific challenge in your team dynamics this period. What contributed to it? *(text)*
9. What is one thing **YOU** will do differently in the next period to be a better teammate? *(text)*

---

## Grading Rubric

The evaluation is graded on the **quality of your reflection**, not on what you say about your teammates or how harshly you rate. Honest, specific feedback at any rating earns full credit.

Each evaluation is worth 10 points.

=== "10 — Exemplary"
    Submitted on time. Every numeric rating is accompanied by a specific behavioral example (not just "Alex is great"). Both open-ended teammate prompts (Q5, Q6) name concrete moments. Self-reflection prompts (Q7–Q9) describe a real incident, identify what contributed to it, and name one concrete action the writer will take. Honest about both strengths and friction.

=== "9 — Strong"
    Submitted on time. Numeric ratings are mostly backed by examples. Open-ended responses are specific. Self-reflection identifies a real moment and a concrete action — but the action is general ("communicate more") rather than situated in a specific behavior.

=== "8 — Solid"
    Submitted on time. Numeric ratings are reasonable but not all are backed by specifics. Open-ended responses for teammates are present and substantive. Self-reflection is honest but reads more like a status update than a reflection — names what happened more than why.

=== "7 — Adequate"
    Submitted on time. Numeric ratings are present but only some have explanations. Open-ended responses are short and generic ("works hard," "good teammate"). Self-reflection is brief and the action item is vague ("try to do better").

=== "6 — Weak"
    Submitted on time, but responses are mostly placeholder text. "Everyone's doing great" with no specifics. Numeric ratings without any explanation. No real self-reflection.

=== "0 — Not submitted"
    Not submitted by the deadline. Or submitted but every text field is blank, "n/a," or a single word.

---

!!! tip "How to write a strong evaluation"
    The difference between a 10 and a 7 is **specificity.** Instead of "works hard," write "Anoushka rewrote the retrieval pipeline over the weekend when it was blocking our sprint — without being asked." Instead of "I'll communicate better," write "I'll send a Teams message by Tuesday each week summarizing what I shipped, so the team isn't surprised at our Friday call."
