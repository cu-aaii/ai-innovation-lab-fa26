# A4 — Tech Lead Check-in Attendance

!!! abstract "Submission details"
    **Type:** Individual · **Weight:** 10% of final grade  
    **Due:** Weekly (best 10 of 12 required)  
    **Format:** No student submission — score recorded in Canvas by your TA  
    **Where:** Canvas → Grades → Tech Lead Check-in Attendance

---

## Overview

Each project team holds a **weekly check-in with your Tech Lead** (separate from the stakeholder sprint meeting). These are your primary touchpoints for technical guidance — where you surface blockers, get architecture feedback, review code, and plan your next sprint.

**Best 10 of 12 attendance grades count toward your final grade.** You have 2 allowed absences.

---

## What These Meetings Cover

- Technical progress update: what you built since the last check-in
- Blockers and architecture questions
- Code review and feedback
- Sprint planning and next-step prioritization
- Tech Lead assessment of individual technical contribution

---

## What to Prepare Each Week

- Know your team's current sprint status (what shipped, what's in progress, what's blocked)
- Have a specific technical question or demo ready
- Come with your own code open and ready to discuss
- Know what you personally committed to last week and whether it's done

---

## Grading Rubric

Each weekly check-in is worth 10 points. Your Tech Lead assigns a tier based on observation; your TA enters it in Canvas. Best 10 of 12 weeks count.

The rubric considers four dimensions together: **punctuality**, **preparation** (knowing your own contribution and the team's status), **technical engagement** (asking real questions, explaining your code, discussing tradeoffs), and **follow-through** (doing what you committed to last week). The score is holistic — pick the tier that best describes the whole check-in.

=== "10 — Exemplary"
    On time. Knows exactly what they built this week and can explain it clearly. Comes with a specific, well-framed technical question or a concrete blocker. Actively engages with Tech Lead feedback — not just listening, but responding, asking follow-ups, and connecting it to their work. Shows clear follow-through on what was agreed last week.

=== "9 — Strong"
    On time. Prepared with their contribution and can speak to team status. Engages substantively with Tech Lead feedback. Has done the work since last check-in and can point to it specifically.

=== "8 — Solid"
    On time. Knows enough to contribute when called on. Listens attentively. Has done work but isn't as specific about their own contribution or how it connects to the sprint goals.

=== "7 — Adequate"
    On time but contributes minimally. When asked to speak, gives a vague update. Doesn't ask technical questions. Follow-through on last week is unclear or unstated.

=== "6 — Weak"
    Arrives 10+ minutes late or leaves early without notice. Or is visibly disengaged (camera off, no contributions). Or clearly hasn't done the work this week and has nothing to show or discuss.

=== "0 — Absent"
    Did not attend. You have 2 excused absences per semester — they function as the 2 dropped scores in the best-10-of-12 calculation.

---

!!! info "If you must miss a check-in"
    Notify your **TA and Tech Lead** at least 2 hours in advance. Pre-notified absences may or may not count toward your attendance grade — check with your TA. This is common courtesy and helps the team plan.
