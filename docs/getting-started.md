## Tips from past cohorts

!!! tip "On the 4-Box report (A2)"
    The weekly 4-Box is most useful when you fill it in **during** your sprint week, not all at once on Friday. Keep a running doc and update it as things ship and break. See the [full template and rubric →](assignments/a2-4box.md)

!!! tip "On stakeholder meetings (A5)"
    These are **client-facing professional meetings.** Your client is a real Cornell stakeholder giving real time to your team. Come prepared every week with a status update, a demo or artifact, and at least one specific question for the client.

!!! tip "On GitHub habits"
    Start clean commit hygiene from Day 1: descriptive commit messages, no secrets committed, no dead branches. Your Tech Lead will review your repo history. The graders for F1 will clone your repo and try to run it from scratch.

!!! tip "On asking for help"
    The rule: ask your teammates first, then your TA, then your Tech Lead. Come with **specific questions** and what you've already tried. "It's not working" is not a question. "I'm getting a 403 on the LiteLLM API after setting the key as an env variable — here's the error and what I've checked" is.
