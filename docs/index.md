## What is this course?

The AI Innovation Lab is a **project studio**. You will not sit in lectures. Instead, you will join a small team and build a real, deployable AI tool for a real Cornell stakeholder — a unit that submitted a problem they actually need solved.

By the end of the semester, your team will have:

- Scoped and shipped a working agentic AI solution
- Presented it to Cornell leadership and your client at a public **Demo Day**
- Handed off a complete package so the client can operate it without you

This is what professional AI engineering looks like.

## Your team

Every student is assigned to a **project team** consisting of:

| Role | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Students (you)** | 2–4 per team; build the tool |
| **Tech Lead** | AI Hub fellow; your technical mentor and sprint partner |
| **Stakeholder / Client** | Cornell unit that submitted the project; your "customer" |
| **TA** | Manages grades, attendance, Canvas, and conflict mediation |
| **Instructor** | Ayham Boucher; office hours for escalations |

Not sure who to ask? **Start with your TA.** They will route you to the right person.
